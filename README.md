## About Me

I am a final-year Data Science student at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC) with a concurrent background in English from the Institute of Foreign Languages (IFL), Royal University of Phnom Penh. 

My work bridges data engineering, machine learning, and natural language processing (NLP), with a focus on building production-style data pipelines and language tools for Khmer.

- **Data Science Degree**: Bachelor of Engineering in Data Science, Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics (AMS), Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC).
- **Languages Degree**: Bachelor of Education in English, Department of English, Institute of Foreign Languages (IFL), Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP).
- **Startup Experience**: Co-founder & NLP Lead at **Akara** (formerly KeyEZ), an AI-powered Khmer typing assistant used by 100+ active users. Top 10 Candidate at Innovative Tech Challenge Season 3.
- **Credentials**: Certified LLM Security Professional (CLLM SP) by Red Team Leaders; ASEAN Data Science Explorers (SAP Analytics Cloud).

## Selected Projects

### 1. [Job Market Analysis Pipeline - Cambodia](https://github.com/menghong-heng/job-market-analysis)
- Built an automated end-to-end ETL pipeline scraping 12,000+ public postings across 13 Cambodian job portals (LinkedIn, BongThom, CamHR, etc.).
- Modeled a relational star-schema in PostgreSQL (fact, dimension, and summary layers) for analytical querying.
- Connected interactive Power BI dashboards visualizing salary trends, top in-demand technical skills, and regional role distributions.
- *Tech Stack*: Python, BeautifulSoup, Pandas, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Power BI, Docker.

### 2. [Trustpilot Sentiment Scraper & Classifier](https://github.com/menghong-heng/beautiful_soup_scrape)
- Extracted consumer reviews using BeautifulSoup and engineered a TF-IDF text representation pipeline.
- Trained a multi-class Logistic Regression classifier achieving 77.7% test accuracy (0.78 weighted F1 score) across positive, neutral, and negative classes.
- Packaged and deployed a live Streamlit web application for real-time inference on arbitrary user-provided text.
- *Tech Stack*: Python, Scikit-learn, Streamlit, Joblib.
